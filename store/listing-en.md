## Description (up to 10,000 characters)

LinkVault is a simple, fast place to keep the links you actually want to come back to.
Instead of dozens of tabs and bookmarks scattered across browsers, you get one table
where every link has a picture and a note next to it.

**What you get**

• A link table: URL, screenshot and description in a single row.
• Pages as categories — "AI tools", "Study", "Work" — switch tabs, rename them, add
  new ones.
• Screenshots: click a cell and press Ctrl+V to keep a preview of the page next to the link.
• Duplicate links are highlighted automatically.
• Drag rows to reorder, right-click a row number to delete.
• JSON backups: "Save" writes a file with all your data, "Load" restores it here or on
  another computer.
• 22 interface languages, including English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese,
  Turkish, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

**Privacy**

LinkVault sends nothing anywhere. No server, no account, no analytics, no ads, no
trackers. Data stays in your browser's local database on your device, and a backup is
just a file on your disk.

**Who it is for**

Anyone collecting useful links: articles, courses, AI tools, videos, shops, references —
for study, work or personal lists.

**Works offline**

Once installed, LinkVault opens without an internet connection and takes almost no
disk space.
