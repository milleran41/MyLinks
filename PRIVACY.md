# Privacy Policy — LinkVault

Last updated: 26 November 2026

LinkVault is a local link manager. It does not have a server, an account system, or any
network features.

## What LinkVault stores

- Links, descriptions, page names and screenshots that you type or paste into the app.
- The interface language you pick.

All of this is stored by your browser in its local database (IndexedDB) and in
`localStorage`, on your own device only.

## What LinkVault sends

Nothing. LinkVault makes no network requests of its own, contains no analytics, no
telemetry, no advertising, no cookies and no third-party scripts. The only network
activity is the browser fetching the page itself when the app is opened from a website
or installed from the Microsoft Store.

## Backups

The `Save` button creates a JSON file with your data and saves it where you choose.
That file is created on your device and is never uploaded anywhere. You control where
it is stored and who can see it.

## Deleting your data

Delete records or pages inside the app, or clear the site data in your browser
(Settings → Privacy → Site data). Removing the installed app from Windows deletes the
app data stored for it. A copy of your data exists only in backup files that you created
yourself.

## Third parties

LinkVault has no third-party services, SDKs or trackers. Links you open from the table
open in your browser and are then covered by the privacy policy of the site you visit.

## Children

LinkVault does not collect any personal data, from anyone.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at
<https://github.com/milleran41/MyLinks/issues>.

# Политика конфиденциальности — LinkVault

LinkVault — локальный менеджер ссылок. У приложения нет сервера, учётных записей и
сетевых функций.

- Ссылки, описания, названия страниц и скриншоты, которые вы вводите, хранятся только
  на вашем устройстве: в локальной базе браузера (IndexedDB) и в `localStorage`.
- Приложение не отправляет данные в интернет, не содержит аналитики, телеметрии,
  рекламы, cookie и сторонних скриптов.
- Резервная копия (`Сохранить`) — это JSON-файл, который создаётся на вашем компьютере
  и никуда не загружается.
- Удалить данные можно внутри приложения, через очистку данных сайта в браузере или
  удалив установленное приложение из Windows.

Вопросы: <https://github.com/milleran41/MyLinks/issues>.
